Can AI replace field capture?

I rode a bike through the University of Canterbury with a 360° camera on my helmet and turned the video into a 3D model. Then I gave one photo from the same video to an AI tool and let it make its own 3D scene. Look at both. The real one shows what was there. The AI one shows what it thinks was there.

1. Real model 2. AI model 3. On the map

1

Real model, built from the video

Made from 328 photos taken along the route. Everything you see was actually filmed.

Open in new tab

Drag to look around. Scroll to zoom.

AI model, built from one photo

Made by Marble (World Labs) from a single 360° photo. Anything the camera did not see, the AI made up.

3

The real model on the map

The real model placed in its true location using phone GPS. The yellow line is the bike route. The AI model cannot do this, because it has no real size or location.

Drag to move. Right-drag or Ctrl + drag to tilt. Scroll to zoom.
